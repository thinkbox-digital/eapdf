# Privacy policy

Updated 13 November 2026.

- **Local files:** EA-PDF processes email and PDFs on your computer. It does not upload them to Thinkbox.DIGITAL or collect analytics or advertising data.
- **Viewers:** local files stay on your device. Opening a web PDF contacts that website only when you ask. The full viewer may save a display preference locally.
- **Gmail:** connecting to Google requires your permission and uses read-only access. Tokens and any optional resume cache are saved on your computer.
- **Saved data:** exports, logs and reports can contain private information. Delete these, tokens and caches when no longer needed. Isolated exports are not anonymized.
- **Website:** GitHub hosts the documentation and downloads and may record visits under its [privacy policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Keep private email out of public issues. [Report security problems privately](https://github.com/thinkbox-digital/eapdf/security/advisories/new).
