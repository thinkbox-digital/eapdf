Creator and validator **2.4.0-beta.1**. Full and Even More Lite readers **1.6.0-beta.1**.

- **Bug fixes.** The creator recovers some mislabeled email text and renders tables that span pages more reliably. The validator catches trailing PDF data and shows clearer collection reports.
- **Speed improvement.** Gmail individual PDFs can convert while the next messages download. The desktop creator shows completed and total message counts.
- **Reader improvements.** Both readers fix identification of affected embedded CJK fonts. Full keeps email navigation in EA-PDF collections, and Even More Lite moves pane and page controls closer to the content they control.

The desktop apps need Java 21 or later. Extract the configuration ZIP next to the creator JAR. Browser extensions are unsigned beta builds. Firefox uses temporary add-on installation.
